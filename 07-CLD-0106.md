**Short Description:**

Duplicate alert associated with previously identified malware infection on LDN-WS-117

**Description:**

Rule **"Defender daily digest - unresolved threats"** fired.

The alert identified **Trojan:Win32/Stealgen.B** on **LDN-WS-117**.

The affected device was assigned to **liam.doyle@cloudora.io**.

Evidence indicated that the threat remained unresolved.

---

## Investigation Strategy

Before beginning the investigation, I considered two possible explanations.

**Malicious hypothesis:**

A new malware infection had been detected on the endpoint.

**Benign hypothesis:**

The alert was a duplicate notification associated with an incident that had already been identified and was currently being remediated.

---

## Evidence Reviewed

The first step was comparing the alert details against previously investigated incidents.

The malware family matched **Trojan:Win32/Stealgen.B**, which immediately stood out because I had already investigated the same threat in **CLD-0102**.

I compared the affected endpoint, assigned user, malware family, and detection details.

The comparison showed that both alerts involved:

- The same malware family
- The same endpoint (**LDN-WS-117**)
- The same user (**liam.doyle@cloudora.io**)

## Investigation Process

My objective was determining whether this represented a second malware infection or an additional notification associated with an existing incident.

I reviewed the documentation from **CLD-0102**.

That investigation confirmed an active credential-stealing malware infection that required endpoint isolation and escalation.

The current alert didn't provide any new indicators, introduce a new host, or identify additional malicious activity.

Instead, it referenced the same unresolved infection that had already been escalated.

At that point, I ruled out the possibility of a separate incident.

---

## Verdict

**Duplicate**

The alert was determined to be a duplicate notification associated with the malware incident documented in **CLD-0102**.

---

## Severity

### Informational — Duplicate alert associated with an existing investigation.

**Why this severity was assigned:**

The alert didn't represent a new compromise.

The underlying malware infection remained serious, but the investigation had already been documented, escalated, and assigned for remediation.

---

## Response Actions

- Linked the alert to CLD-0102.
- Documented the duplicate relationship.
- Closed the ticket.
- Continued tracking remediation through the original incident.

---

## Justification

The malware family, endpoint, user, and detection details all matched the previously investigated incident.

No new evidence suggested additional malicious activity.

The alert represented duplicate reporting rather than a separate security event.

---

## MITRE ATT&CK

**T1003 – OS Credential Dumping**

The MITRE mapping remained unchanged because the duplicate alert referenced the same credential-stealing malware identified in CLD-0102.
